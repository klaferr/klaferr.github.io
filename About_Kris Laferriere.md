# About

Planetary scientist studying volatile mass balance across the Solar System

![Kris standing in front of Kerid Crater in Iceland, May 2024](https://klaferr.github.io/images/main/KrisIceland2024.jpeg)

At Kerid Crater, Iceland, May 2024.

I’m a planetary science Ph.D. working as a postdoctoral scholar at the Florida Space Institute in Orlando, Florida, associated with the University of Central Florida. I’m part of the planetary radar group, working on asteroid shape modeling using Arecibo data.

Previously, I was a Ph.D. student at Purdue University working with [Professor Ali Bramson](https://www.eaps.purdue.edu/bramson/), studying the volatile mass balance on the Moon and Mars.

## Research interests

I'm broadly interested in planetary surface processes, with a special focus on the presence of ice, on bodies across the Solar System. I combine observations, such as subsurface sounding radar and near-IR spectra, with modeling, such as 1D thermal models and ballistic transport models.
